Cookies Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains browser storage and similar technologies used by UK IT Jobs. The exact technologies can change as site features and third-party services change.

Technologies used by the site

  • Local storage: the website uses browser local storage to remember saved jobs on your device. This is not a traditional cookie.
  • Authentication: Supabase and Google sign-in may use cookies or similar browser storage to maintain your sign-in session and security.
  • Analytics: the site loads Counter.dev analytics. Check the provider's current documentation to confirm exactly what data and storage it uses.

Managing browser storage

You can clear cookies and site data through your browser settings. Clearing storage may sign you out or remove saved jobs stored on this device.

Updates

We will update this policy if the website's use of cookies or similar technologies changes. Questions can be sent via our Contact page.
