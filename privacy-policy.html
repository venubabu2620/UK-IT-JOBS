Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This page explains how UK IT Jobs ("we", "us") handles information when you use this website. Please review it before using the service.

Information we may process

  • Sign-in: if you sign in with Google, Supabase authentication processes account information such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Saved jobs: saved-job choices are stored in your browser's local storage on this device.
  • Technical/usage data: the site currently loads a Counter.dev analytics script. That service may process technical usage information according to its own policy.
  • Contact messages: if you email us, we receive the information you include in your message.

How we use information

We use information to operate sign-in, remember saved jobs on your device, understand site usage, respond to enquiries, and maintain the website. We do not ask you to submit sensitive personal information.

Third-party services and job links

Authentication is provided through Supabase and Google. Job listings may link to third-party employer or recruitment websites. Those services have their own privacy practices and terms; review them before sharing information or applying.

Retention and your choices

Saved jobs can be cleared from your browser storage. You can sign out of the website and manage Google account permissions through your Google account. Browser controls can manage local storage and cookies, though some site features may stop working.

Children and security

This website is intended for general job-seeking audiences and is not designed to collect information from children. We take reasonable steps to keep the site working securely, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, use the Contact page. We may update this notice as the website changes.

Site owner note: This is a starter policy, not legal advice. Before publishing, confirm the actual analytics/cookie behaviour, identify the data controller and contact email, and obtain appropriate UK privacy advice for your circumstances.
